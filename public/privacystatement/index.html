Skunkworks Academy Portal

Privacy Statement

Effective date: May 28, 2026

This page explains the general privacy position for the Skunkworks Academy Portal.

Purpose

The Portal supports academy operations, communication, onboarding workflow and service administration.

Systems

The Portal may use Microsoft identity services, Azure Functions, Microsoft Graph and SharePoint Online.

Access

Administrative access is limited to authorized Skunkworks Academy users.

Requests

Requests about Portal records can be raised with Skunkworks Academy.
