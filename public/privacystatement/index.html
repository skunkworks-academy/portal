Skunkworks Academy Portal

Privacy Statement

Effective date: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Statement explains how Skunkworks Academy processes personal information submitted through the Instructor Portal.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, Microsoft account identifier, teaching discipline, availability, experience, application status, uploaded documents, and related onboarding records.

How We Use Information

We use portal information to manage instructor recruitment, review applications, conduct onboarding, prepare contracts, maintain operational records, and communicate with applicants and instructors.

Microsoft 365 and SharePoint

Portal records may be stored in Microsoft 365 services, including Microsoft Entra ID, Azure Functions, Microsoft Graph, and SharePoint Online. Access to administrative records is restricted to authorized Skunkworks Academy personnel.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed for recruitment, onboarding, compliance, academy operations, and legitimate business recordkeeping, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Choices

You may request correction or deletion of your information where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to raydo@skunkworks.africa.
