Trust and governance
Responsible connections by design.
Introductions should be relevant, consent-based, commercially appropriate and limited to the information required for the stated purpose.
Privacy first
Member details are not published as a public directory. Contact information is shared only after permission and suitability checks.
Clear boundaries
Requests must state the purpose, authority, scope, geography, commercial context and any data-handling constraints.
Evidence-aware
Delivery, talent and project matches may require verified credentials, portfolios, references, assessments or proof of competence.

Frequently asked questions
Before requesting a connection.
Is this a public member directory?
No. The page is a structured routing and introduction service. It does not expose private member records or contact details.
Can employers request assessed candidates?
Yes. Provide the role profile, competency requirements, location, work arrangement, evidence standard and selection timeline.
Can instructors join regional delivery teams?
Yes. Include delivery topics, certifications, languages, modality, regions, dates, references and practical evidence.
Can technology partners request programme collaboration?
Yes. Define the target market, products, credentials, labs, campaign, commercial model, governance and intended learner outcome.
