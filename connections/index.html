Trust and governance
Connection standards by design.
The page is a routing layer, not a public member directory. Personal contact details are not exposed through the catalogue.
Consent first
Introductions proceed only when the relevant participants agree to the connection.
Data minimisation
Share only the information needed to evaluate and act on the request.
Evidence-led matching
Use credentials, portfolios, role requirements and delivery evidence to improve fit.
Abuse controls
Unsolicited marketing, scraping, harassment and unauthorised contact sharing are not permitted.


Frequently asked questions
Before requesting a connection.
Does this page expose learner, instructor or partner contact details?
No. The connection catalogue describes pathways and routes requests through the Academy. Contact details should be exchanged only after consent.
Can I request an instructor or subject-matter expert for a delivery requirement?
Yes. Use the connection planner and include the topic, level, delivery dates, location, modality, audience size and required evidence or certification.
Can employers request assessed learners or graduate talent?
Yes. Describe the role, competency requirements, location, work arrangement and selection process. Talent referrals remain subject to learner consent and readiness.
How are regional requests handled?
Regional requests are reviewed for local capacity, language, delivery mode, legal requirements, commercial fit and participant availability before an introduction is made.
